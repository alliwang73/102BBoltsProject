BoltSorter wiring

Adafruit Feather ESP32 V2, two DRV8833 drivers, three Pololu #2215 gearmotors with encoders, and one feeder servo. The pin numbers match BoltSorter.ino.

Feather pinout

The header pins in physical order, top of the board first (USB end). A coloured pin is used; grey is free.

Drivers, motors and power

Each motor runs off one DRV8833 channel. Its encoder goes straight back to the Feather and runs on 3.3 V. All grounds join on one rail.

Three rules that prevent most problems

Encoders on 3V, never 6 V

Their A/B outputs swing to whatever VCC is, and ESP32 pins are 3.3 V only.

One shared ground

Feather, both drivers, both supplies, encoders, servo and switches. Without it, counts and PWM go erratic.

Servo on its own 5 V

A servo stall can brown out the Feather. Use a 5 V BEC or supply; the USB pin is fine only for bench tests.

Connection tables

Optional inputs (limit switches, bolt sensor) are wired switch-to-ground and use internal pull-ups; turn them on from the control page. If an axis runs or counts backwards, tick Reverse motor or Reverse encoder on the control page instead of rewiring. Free pins for later: 12, 34, 36, 37, 39 (34–39 input-only, 12 must be low at boot).
